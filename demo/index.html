Inspired by Neural characteristic mapping (arXiv:2610.12395) by Martin Campos Pinto, Victor Fournet, Emmanuel Franck, Philipp Krah, and Victor Michel-Dansac. This demo uses a classical flow-map idea to carry initial detail through a shared live fluid field; it does not use the paper’s neural network or reproduce its training method.
